How do you store a circle in a computer? Not as a circle. This episode builds a cubic Bézier curve from nothing but repeated linear interpolation (de Casteljau's construction), then shows how anchors and handles join curves smoothly and why cubics are the standard choice.

A Manim outline turns out to be a list of points in groups of four, and that explains how one shape morphs into another: matching point counts, subdivision, and path_arc.
