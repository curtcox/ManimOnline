A dot becomes a line, a circle, a square, a star, and every frame of it is a few lines of Python. This is Manim, the library behind a whole genre of animated math explanations, and ManimOnline, which runs a Manim-compatible engine inside your web browser: no install, no server.

Thirteen episodes take you from your first scene to cameras, 3D, graphs and formulas, then under the hood of the editor itself. Scan the code at the end to edit this trailer's final scene.
