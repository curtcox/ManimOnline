Everything you see in a Manim scene is a mobject. This episode builds the vocabulary: Manim's coordinate plane and direction constants, a zoo of shapes, stroke, fill and opacity, placing things absolutely and relative to each other, scaling, rotating and measuring.

Then the parts that surprise people: groups that move as one, two names for the same object (and when to copy), and who is drawn on top.
