Deep dive 2: ManimOnline can't run real Manim in the browser, so it brings its own engine, manim-lite. How do you know it's right? This episode defines compatibility as observable behaviour that has to be measured, and shows four ways manim-lite is compared with Manim Community, frame for frame.

Along the way: surprising behaviours a faithful reimplementation must copy, and a clear statement of what manim-lite is not.
