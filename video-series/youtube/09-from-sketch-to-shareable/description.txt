A practical tour of the editor for building something real. Find and load examples in the Sample gallery, choose a scene in a file with several, and understand automatic rendering, Render animation, Cancel render and the limits of the preview.

Then export frames and MP4 or WebM videos, share with a link or save permanently with HashBin, work offline, meet the Graphviz and modl modes, build larger scenes in small checkpointed steps, and know when to move to a full Manim install.
