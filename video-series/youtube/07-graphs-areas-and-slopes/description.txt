Number lines, axes and plots, built up to calculus you can watch. This episode converts numbers to points, builds Axes and converts with c2p, plots parametric curves and functions (and explains that plots are sampled), and labels them.

Then the payoff: Riemann rectangles converging to an area, an area with a moving bound, a secant line becoming a tangent as dx shrinks, and what a matrix does to the whole plane.
