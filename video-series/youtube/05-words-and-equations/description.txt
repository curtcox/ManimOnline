Text and formulas in Manim are shapes too. This episode chooses between Text, MathTex and Tex, shows how a formula becomes vector outlines (in Manim with LaTeX, and in ManimOnline with MathJax), and indexes letters and formula parts to colour and animate them separately.

Then algebra that moves: TransformMatchingTex, aligned lines of working, braces, titles and TransformFromCopy.
