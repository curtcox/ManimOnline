The camera is part of the scene. This episode pans and zooms with MovingCameraScene, shows a magnified inset with ZoomedScene, then steps into 3D: placing the camera with phi and theta, ThreeDAxes, surfaces and solids, and text that stays flat.

It closes with how to keep a 3D scene readable, and how a 3D frame becomes a flat picture.
