Every Manim animation is interpolation driven by one number that runs from 0 to 1. Once you see that, rate functions, built-in animations and timing all make sense.

This episode explains what self.play really does, compares linear, smooth and there-and-back motion, tours the built-in animations, explains why .animate.rotate and Rotate differ (and why a square can shrink to a point), combines animations with AnimationGroup, LaggedStart and Succession, and settles who is on screen after a Transform.
