Some things in a scene should follow others: a label that sticks to a dot, a readout that tracks a value, a path traced as something moves. This episode attaches updaters that run every frame, writes time-based updaters with dt, and drives several objects from one ValueTracker.

It also covers always_redraw and TracedPath, the common updater surprises, and how to choose between updaters, trackers, become and plain animations.
