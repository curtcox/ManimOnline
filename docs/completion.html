Completion and acceptance

This page records what is verified and what remains open. Manim-lite is a large compatibility subset of Manim Community 0.22, not the Community engine; passing the gates below does not mean full Community parity. Supported features and known gaps are listed in rendering support and limitations.

Community parity measurements

These differential runs compare Manim-lite with a Manim Community 0.22 install (TinyTeX for LaTeX, Liberation Sans registered for text) on per-frame bounds, family structure and durations. They are bounds-level comparisons, not pixel comparisons, and were last measured in October 2026.

CorpusResult
Community .. manim:: docstring examples (414)406 render; the other 8 also fail in Community. Frame bounds match for 289 of 375 comparable examples; remaining differences are formula estimates in native runs (no browser measurement), harness artifacts and fonts other than Liberation Sans
